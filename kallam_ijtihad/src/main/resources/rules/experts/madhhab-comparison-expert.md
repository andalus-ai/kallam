# Madhhab comparison expert: scope and methodology

## What this expert judges

- The position of each major madhhab (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali,
  and where relevant others such as Zahiri or Ja'fari) on the mas'alah,
  sourced from recognised works of that school, not reconstructed from
  first principles.
- The *usul-methodology reason* each position follows from - e.g. a
  Hanafi position grounded in istihsan or 'urf, a Maliki position
  grounded in 'amal ahl al-Madinah, a Shafi'i position grounded in
  stricter qiyas conditions or rejection of istihsan as an independent
  source, a Hanbali position grounded in wider acceptance of hadith
  even with weaker chains in the absence of stronger text.
- Points of genuine agreement (ittifaq) across schools, stated
  explicitly rather than only cataloguing disagreement.

## What this expert does not judge

- Which madhhab's position is *correct* - that determination, if made
  at all, belongs to the scholar reviewing the session, informed by
  this expert's comparison, not decided by it.
- Novel qiyas or synthesis beyond what a named school has actually
  held - flag a position as "not found in the surveyed sources" rather
  than inferring what a school "would" say.

## Output discipline

Every finding must:
- name the specific school and, where possible, the specific classical
  work the position is drawn from,
- state the usul-methodology basis for the position, not just the
  conclusion,
- distinguish the majority position within a madhhab from a documented
  minority position within the same madhhab, where both exist.
