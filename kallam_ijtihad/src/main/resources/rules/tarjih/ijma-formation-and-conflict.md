# Ijma' al-sahabah: formation and conflict

This tier is reached only when Qur'an and hadith are both genuinely
silent on the mas'alah (per the tier-gating rule). A claimed ijma' should
be treated with real scrutiny before it is allowed to govern a
determination.

## Before accepting a claimed ijma'

1. **Is it explicit (sarih) or tacit (sukuti)?** Explicit ijma' - a
   recorded agreement or an absence of any transmitted dissent on a
   matter that was widely known and discussed - carries more weight than
   tacit ijma' inferred from silence, since silence is also consistent
   with a matter simply not having been raised.

2. **Is the transmission of "no dissent" itself reliable?** A claim of
   ijma' is only as strong as the historical record that no companion is
   known to have disagreed. If dissenting positions are documented from
   even a minority of sahabah, this is not ijma' - it is a majority
   position among the sahabah, which is a different (weaker) kind of
   evidence and should be labelled as such rather than reported as
   consensus.

3. **Scope precisely what the ijma' actually covers.** A common error is
   treating consensus on a general principle as consensus on every
   specific application of it. State the exact proposition the ijma'
   is claimed for.

## When two claimed ijma' positions conflict

Genuine ijma' cannot conflict with genuine ijma' on the same point by
definition - if two sources report conflicting "consensus" positions, at
least one claim is not actually ijma' (more likely a majority or
individual position mislabelled). Resolve by:

1. Re-examining each claim against criteria 1-3 above; the weaker-attested
   claim is very likely not ijma' at all and should be relabelled and
   demoted (it may still be a valid madhhab position, just not at this
   tier).
2. If both claims survive scrutiny and a genuine conflict remains, this
   itself is strong evidence that neither is sound ijma' - record as
   unresolved and note the specific documented dissent.

## Scoring guidance for the expert agent

- +35 for explicit (sarih) ijma' with a named, checkable source
  compilation (e.g. a recognised ijma' work), versus inferred/tacit
- +20 for absence of any documented minority dissent after a genuine
  search, not merely an absence of search
- -40 for treating a majority-of-sahabah position as ijma' without
  qualification
- -25 for claiming ijma' on a specific application when the sourced
  agreement was only on a general principle
