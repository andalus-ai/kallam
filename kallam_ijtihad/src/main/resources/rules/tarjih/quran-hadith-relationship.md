# Qur'an-hadith relationship: conflict or clarification?

Most apparent Qur'an-hadith conflicts are not conflicts - the hadith is
functioning as bayan (explanation/elaboration) of the Qur'an, which is
one of the Sunnah's recognised roles. Check that before treating the
two as genuinely in tension.

1. **Is the hadith actually qualifying, specifying, or explaining the
   ayah**, rather than contradicting it? A hadith that specifies (khass)
   a general ayah, or explains an ambiguous (mujmal) one, is consistent
   with the Qur'an by design - this is not a conflict requiring tarjih at
   all.

2. **If a genuine conflict remains** after step 1, and the hadith is
   ahad (not mutawatir), the Qur'an - being definitively established in
   transmission (qat'i al-thubut) - takes precedence over an ahad
   hadith's *transmission-level* certainty. This does not by itself mean
   the hadith is rejected outright; consider first whether the apparent
   Qur'an ruling itself has been read in the widest sense the text
   actually supports, or whether a narrower, textually justified reading
   removes the conflict.

3. **A mutawatir hadith genuinely conflicting with an ayah** is a
   different and much rarer case; whether it can function as naskh of a
   Qur'anic ruling is a disputed usul question across madhahib (the
   Hanafi position differs from the Shafi'i position here). Do not
   resolve this automatically - flag for scholar review with both
   positions stated, citing the applicable madhhab.

4. If the conflict cannot be resolved by 1-3, record as unresolved
   ikhtilaf, same as the Qur'an-Qur'an case.

## Scoring guidance for the expert agent

- +40 if the hadith's role as bayan/takhsis is textually supportable
  rather than assumed
- +25 for independent confirmation of the hadith's grading (mutawatir
  vs ahad, and if ahad, its sahih/hasan/da'if status)
- -30 if a hadith is treated as contradicting the Qur'an without first
  checking whether it is functioning as explanation
