# Qiyas as last resort

Qiyas is not a fourth, co-equal source alongside Qur'an, Sunnah, and
ijma' - it is what a mujtahid turns to only once those three are
genuinely exhausted for the mas'alah at hand. This is the majority usul
position across the four Sunni madhahib, though the Zahiri school rejects
qiyas as a source altogether, and madhahib differ on how liberally
istihsan or other supplementary reasoning may substitute for or qualify
strict qiyas.

## Before qiyas may be invoked

Confirm, and record in the audit trail, that each of the following is
true - not assumed:

1. No ayah in the Qur'an addresses this mas'alah, directly or through a
   textually-supported general principle that plainly covers it.
2. No sahih or hasan hadith addresses it either, and no da'if hadith is
   being relied upon in the absence of corroboration (a weak hadith does
   not by itself move a mas'alah out of the qiyas tier, but it also does
   not settle it).
3. No sound ijma' al-sahabah, explicit or tacit, has been transmitted on
   the point.

If any of the above turns out to be false on closer research, escalate
back up the tier cascade - do not proceed with a qiyas-tier finding
alongside an unresolved higher-tier one.

## Arkan al-qiyas (pillars) - required for validity once qiyas is reached

- **Asl** (the origin case): must itself have an established hukm from
  Qur'an, Sunnah, or ijma' - qiyas cannot be built on another qiyas's
  conclusion as its asl (this is disputed among usuliyyun; treat qiyas
  al-'illah chains as requiring explicit scholarly justification, not a
  default).
- **Far'** (the branch case): the mas'alah actually under research.
- **Hukm al-asl**: the ruling attached to the asl.
- **'Illah**: the effective cause. It must be:
  - present in both the asl and the far' (if absent from either, the
    qiyas fails outright),
  - **mundabit** - a controlled, objectively identifiable attribute, not
    a vague or unmeasurable one,
  - **muta'addin** - transitive, i.e. capable of extending beyond the
    single case of the asl (an illah proper to the asl alone, qasr,
    cannot support qiyas),
  - not restricted to the asl by the text itself (if the nass explicitly
    limits its ruling's scope, extending it by qiyas contradicts the
    nass's own stated scope rather than reasoning from it).

## Scoring guidance for the expert agent

Score a proposed qiyas 0-100 for *formal* validity (this is not a claim
about whether the conclusion is correct, only whether the analogy is
properly constructed):

- +30 the illah is explicitly stated in the text (mansusah) rather than
  extracted (mustanbatah)
- +20 the illah is mundabit with no plausible alternative reading
- +20 no competing illah has been proposed for the same asl by another
  expert or a classical source
- +15 the far' shares the illah without also sharing a disqualifying
  difference (farq mu'aththir) from the asl
- -40 the illah is only asserted, not independently justified from the
  text or context
- -25 a plausible farq mu'aththir (an operative difference between asl
  and far') has not been addressed
